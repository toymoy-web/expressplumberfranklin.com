Privacy Policy

Franklin Express Plumbing ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain terms, how information submitted to us through this website or by phone is used.

We collect only the information you provide directly, such as your name, phone number, and a description of your plumbing issue, when you call us or otherwise contact us. We use this information solely to respond to your request and provide the service you've asked for.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Call recordings, where applicable, may be used for quality and training purposes.

This website uses standard analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. No sensitive personal information is collected through analytics.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us using the information on our Contact page.
