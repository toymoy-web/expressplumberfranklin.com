Running Toilet

A flapper that doesn't seal or a fill valve out of adjustment.

Local Context

Plumbing Repair In Franklin, TN

These smaller repairs add up, and they're also the easiest ones to put off. A running toilet or a dripping faucet isn't urgent the way a burst pipe is, but it's also the kind of thing that's worth handling on a normal weekday call rather than waiting for it to become a bigger problem.

A fair number of repair calls in Franklin's older homes trace back to fixtures and valves that simply haven't been touched in fifteen or twenty years. A shutoff valve under a sink that's never been closed can seize up from mineral buildup, and when a homeowner finally needs to close it during an emergency, it either won't turn or leaks once it does. Exercising shutoff valves periodically is a small maintenance habit that avoids a much worse moment later.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is a dripping faucet worth fixing right away?

Yes, a slow drip wastes real water over a year and often signals a worn part that's easy to replace now and harder later.

Why does my toilet run intermittently?

Usually a flapper that isn't sealing fully, letting water slowly leak from the tank into the bowl until it triggers a refill.

Can you match older fixture parts?

In most cases, yes. When an exact part isn't available, we present a compatible replacement option before doing any work.

Do you offer same-day repair calls?

Yes, most standard repair calls are scheduled the same day.
