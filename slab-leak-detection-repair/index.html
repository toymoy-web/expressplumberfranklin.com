Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Franklin, TN

A slab leak rarely announces itself with a burst or a flood. It shows up as a warm patch on the floor near the edge of a room, a water bill that's climbed for no obvious reason, or a faint sound of running water when every fixture in the house is off. We trace the leak to its exact location with electronic detection equipment before recommending any repair, so a homeowner isn't paying for exploratory demolition across a slab to find something a listening device could pinpoint in an hour.

Common Question

How Do I Know If It's Actually A Slab Leak?

This is the question we hear most on these calls, usually phrased as "I have a leak somewhere but I don't know where it's coming from." The combination that points most clearly to a slab leak is a rising water bill plus a specific warm or damp spot on the floor, particularly near where a hot water line would run. Not every unexplained bill increase is a slab leak, a running toilet or an irrigation line left on can look similar, so we rule out the easy explanations first. When those don't account for it, electronic detection narrows the leak to a precise spot before anyone starts cutting into flooring.

Local Context

Slab Leak Detection & Repair In Franklin, TN

This is one of the more common calls we get from Franklin's newer subdivisions built from the 1990s through the 2010s, where slab construction sits directly on the same shifting clay-limestone soil discussed throughout this site. It's not a sign of poor construction. It's a predictable outcome of building on this specific ground, and catching the early signs, the warm spot, the climbing bill, before it becomes a bigger repair is mostly a matter of knowing what to watch for.

Copper lines under a slab tend to develop pinhole leaks at a specific weak point rather than failing along their whole length, which is part of why precise detection matters so much here. A homeowner who's had one slab leak repaired sometimes assumes the whole line is compromised and starts planning a full repipe, when in many cases the rest of the copper run is sound and a targeted repair is the more honest recommendation.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is a slab leak an emergency?

It's rarely sudden, but it worsens steadily and can eventually stress the foundation. Worth addressing promptly once suspected, not necessarily a same-hour emergency.

Do you charge for the detection visit separately from the repair?

We quote detection and repair as connected steps, with the final repair price confirmed once the leak location is known.

Can you fix it without tearing up my whole floor?

In most cases, yes. Precise detection is what keeps the repair small and targeted instead of an exploratory cut.

Will my insurance cover a slab leak?

Coverage varies by policy and cause. Sudden failures are sometimes covered, gradual ones often are not. Check your specific policy.
