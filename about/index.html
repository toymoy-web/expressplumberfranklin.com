About Franklin Express Plumbing

Franklin Express Plumbing is a Tennessee-licensed plumbing company serving Franklin, Brentwood, and Spring Hill in Williamson County. We're built around two things: diagnosing the real cause of a plumbing problem before recommending a fix, and giving a flat, honest price before any work begins.

Franklin sits on genuinely distinct ground compared to a lot of markets. The clay-limestone karst soil under most of Williamson County drives real, recurring patterns, basement water intrusion after heavy spring rain, slab leaks in homes built on that same shifting soil, and sinkhole risk in parts of the broader region, that a plumber unfamiliar with the area might not connect to a simple wet-wall or high-water-bill call. We treat that local knowledge as part of the job, not an afterthought.

Franklin's housing stock is also a genuine mix: historic homes in and around the downtown core, some running original galvanized or aging supply lines, alongside newer planned-community construction in Westhaven and Berry Farms with different plumbing needs entirely. We size every recommendation, from a water heater repair to a full repipe, to the specific home in front of us rather than a generic template.

Every job starts with an honest diagnosis and ends with a flat price agreed to before any work begins. We don't invent problems to upsell, and if a repair genuinely makes more sense than a replacement, that's what we recommend.
