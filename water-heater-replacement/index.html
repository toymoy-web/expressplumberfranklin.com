Local Context

Water Heater Replacement In Franklin, TN

We size every replacement to the household, not just the footprint of the old tank. A three-bedroom home in Brentwood running two bathrooms and a dishwasher on the same schedule every morning needs more real capacity than the 40-gallon unit that came with the house a decade ago. Walking through actual usage before quoting a size is how we avoid selling a tank that leaves someone with a cold shower six months from now.

Franklin's Building Department generally requires a permit for water heater replacement when the work changes venting, gas line sizing, or moves the unit's location, but a like-for-like tank swap in the same spot is typically a more straightforward process. We confirm what a specific job actually requires before quoting, rather than defaulting to the most complicated version of the job.

For tankless conversions specifically, the real question is almost always the existing gas line. A standard 40-gallon tank runs on a 1/2 inch gas line in most Franklin homes, while a tankless unit generating hot water on demand typically needs a 3/4 inch line to deliver enough BTU output, especially for larger tankless models sized for a full household. We check the existing line size on-site before quoting a tankless conversion, since an undersized line that isn't upgraded is the most common reason a new tankless installation underperforms.
